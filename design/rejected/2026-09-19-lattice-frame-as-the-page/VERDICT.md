## His verdict, verbatim

## Why they are here and not deleted

They are not wrong, they are *narrow*. All four optimise inside a single idea —
the lattice frame as the page — and vary only in how much of the page is field.
That is a degree axis, not a kind axis. The verdict is about the bar, not about
which of the four sits at the right point on it.

`plans-source-directions.py` is `generators/directions.py` as it stood when
these were built, so any of the four can be rebuilt exactly.
